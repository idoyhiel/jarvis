Ido's Jarvis

A private personal assistant used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public.

Privacy policy

The app reads the owner's Gmail with read-only access in order to summarize and search the owner's own email. Email content is sent to the Anthropic Claude API for processing and is not shared with anyone else, sold, or used for advertising. The app does not send, modify, or delete email. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
